# Track F: hard 2 s per-query timeout (300 / 500 atoms)

Harness: `scripts/pettachainer/pc_timeout.py`. A pre-warmed worker subprocess loads the seeded rules, mem_inh, N atoms
(same construction as `pc_scale.py`) and the Grace canary facts. The parent sends one goal at a time; if no result
arrives within `--timeout` s it SIGKILLs the worker's whole process group, records a timeout, and warms a replacement
(warm-up time is not counted). The canary `(Member grace person)` (start and end) and the negative control go through
the same worker/timeout path. Every result is structurally checked before it is recorded. Replay atoms are read from
gitignored `.local-data/`; the raw lines in `timeout_raw/` hold numbers only.

| N | timeout | queries | timeouts | median (completed) | median (timeouts counted as 2 s) | max completed | canary start/end | neg control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | 2 s | 16 (8 goals x2) | 3 (18.8%) | 1.47 s | 1.70 s | 1.94 s | pass / pass | pass |
| 300 | 2 s | 24 (8 goals x3) | 4 (16.7%) | 1.41 s | 1.43 s | 1.96 s | TIMEOUT (2.01 s) / pass | pass (1.995 s) |
| 500 | 2 s | 16 | 16 (100%) | - | 2.01 s | - | timeout / timeout | timeout |
| 500 | 30 s (control) | 8 | 0 | 4.05 s | 4.05 s | 8.29 s | pass / pass | pass |

Pooled at 300: 7/40 timeouts (17.5%); completed queries cluster at 1.4-1.9 s, so 2 s sits just above the median
rather than safely above the tail. At 500 nothing fits in 2 s, but the 30 s control shows the reasoner is correct there
(all 8 goals proved, canary and negative control pass), so it's latency, not breakage.

Verdict: 300 is viable only for a non-blocking shadow mode, where a timeout just means fewer candidates.
It isn't viable for anything on the turn's critical path.
