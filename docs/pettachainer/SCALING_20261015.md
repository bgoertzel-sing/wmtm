## Curve
```
query s (log)   300   500   ~1000   2000        2990        3000
positive        1.4   7.6   18      0.06        0.06        0.06
negative        1.3   5.7   20      80          190         0.06
peak MB          67    91   110     210         400         60
```
Load cost is linear (~3–5 ms/atom; 3000 atoms ≈ 11 s, one-time for a persistent space).
Query cost is **not** a smooth function of N: it grows ~linearly-to-superlinearly up to ~1000 (1.4 → 18 s), then positive queries collapse to 0.06 s at ≥2000 while the failing (negative) query explodes (80 s at 2000, 190 s at 2990) – except at exactly 3000, where everything is 0.06 s.

## What I ruled out
- Not wrong answers: the fast proofs were logged and are the correct `mem_inh` derivations (real and synthetic).
- Not bounded-agenda pruning: at 1000 with `set-bounded-agenda-pruning false` the times are unchanged (18 / 19 s).
- Not the trailing partial copy: re-adding the first 10 or 1 real atoms (renamed) at the end of a 1000 load does not make it fast (19 s).
- Reproducible: 2000, 2990, 3000 and 1000 were each run 2–6 times with the same pattern.
Root cause of the regime switch is **not found** (time-boxed). Candidates: rule specialization (`specialize-rule-from-stored-facts`, compile.metta:718) / index materialization changing with KB contents, or a SWI-Prolog first-argument/JIT index switching on at some table size.

## Verdict
**The ~5 s background-lane design is not viable at 3000 atoms as specified.** Even taking the fast regime at face value, a single failing goal costs 80–200 s at 2000–2990, and we cannot predict which regime a given KB is in. In the slow regime, one query already exceeds the whole 5 s budget from ~500 atoms.
It is viable only if the space reasoned over stays at **≤ ~300 atoms** (≈1.4 s/query, ~3 goals per 5 s cycle): i.e. build/maintain the chainer space from the attention focus set (top-K items), not the whole WM/LTM, and enforce a hard per-query timeout (worker process killed on timeout) so failing goals cannot stall the lane.
Recommended before shadow mode: (a) ask upstream about the regime switch with this harness; (b) measure 300/500 with focus-set-only KBs and a per-query timeout of 2 s.
