# t-2026-11-17-wmtm-variable-implication-test

## Summary
andy asked for a small shadow test of whether PeTTaChainer proves note-stated Implications with variables (for example, "anything acoustic is quiet" plus "the lute is acoustic") when they are loaded the way the parser lowers them. Six invented notes were parsed with the real parser at 24e37182 in sentence mode, loaded through master_loader on PR 43, given note-implication goals, and asked of PeTTaChainer. **The answer is: only in the simplest shape.** "Every bird that sings is happy" plus "Tweety is a bird that sings" gives a goal, and the goal is proved (STV 1.0/0.89, one step). The other four positive shapes give no goal and are not proved. The near miss (a metallic trumpet) does not fire.

The variables are not lost: the lowering keeps `$x` in every rule premise. What blocks the proof is the content around the variables. (1) "Anything …" adds a premise `(Member $x thing)` that no note ever states. (2) "Anything acoustic is quiet" becomes an Inheritance from the narrowed class `thing__member_acoustic`, and the master target has no rule that puts anything into that class. (3) The pronoun in "…, they are loud" becomes a skolem constant `sk_they_1`, not `$x`, although the parser's own reply linked "they" to "someone". (4) "The toy" in a later sentence gets a new skolem joined only by Coref, so "red" and "round" end up on different individuals. PeTTaChainer itself is fine: with two mechanical loader-side additions (diagnostic arm, not the master path), it proves the lute case (STV 0.9/0.7) and the negated pond case ((Member pond liquid) at strength 0.0, confidence 0.89). **Whose fix:** items 1 to 4 belong to the parser's lowering (Man Hin, semantic-parsing-hitl `tmpl/lower.py`, master target). Our loader only calls it and adds nothing. Two smaller items are ours: (a) note_impl_goals matches premises one step against stated facts only, so it never builds a goal that needs any inference; (b) whether to add a stopgap like the diagnostic "everything is a thing" and narrowed-membership atoms before the parser fix lands is a decision for you. Two planned extra cases (a converse near miss, and an instance in another note) were not parsed because the shared task share was full until about 12:41.

## Question
When a note states a general implication with a variable and another sentence gives a matching instance, does the master path (lowering, master_loader, note-implication goals) build a goal, and does PeTTaChainer prove it? Shadow only, invented notes only.

## Answer
**Does PeTTaChainer prove variable implications as lowered?** Only when the lowered premise matches stated facts literally. That held in 1 of 5 positive shapes (C2). The engine is not the blocker (C2, the engine check, and diag C1/C5 are all proved). Variables are **not** skolemised wholesale: every rule keeps `$x` in its premise. They are lost in one place, the pronoun conclusion of C3 (`sk_they_1`). The other failures come from lowering content around the variables.
