**Status:** the 36b3fe6 bug (no And-elimination at all) is fixed in 202b9379. The conjuncts are now derived through
`(node (conjunction c))`, but every answer, including the stored conjunction itself, comes back with confidence 0.

- Expected: the conjuncts have c clearly above 0 (the true posterior gives c≈0.54). The stored And keeps about 0.99, or at least gets c > 0.

## Analysis
`event_graph` turns the stored `(And A B)` into a Pearl factor m=(n·s+1)/(n+2) with n=K·c/(1-c), K=800, so m≈0.99999.
The factor sits over atoms A and B, and both have uniform Beta(1,1) priors. `polynomial_answer` (event_node.pl:82) computes
`Bound = (Q + max(C,1-C)·sqrt(ZVar·Q)) / EZ²` and then sets `V = min(C(1-C), Bound)`. With uniform roots ZVar is large (0.0486)
while Q is tiny, so Bound ends up above s(1-s). V is therefore capped at s(1-s), and `node_stv` maps V ≥ s(1-s) to c=0.
The conjuncts' 3.7e-16 is float rounding left over from the same cap.

Numbers from `pc3_conf_check.py` (exact rational moments plus a 4000² grid posterior):

| goal | mean | s(1-s) | engine bound (c) | Z≥1-m bound (c) | true posterior var (c) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (Member o offer) | 0.99997475 | 2.525e-05 | 2.969e-05 (0) | 2.244e-05 (1.6e-4) | 2.727e-08 (0.54) |
| And | 0.99996212 | 3.788e-05 | 3.928e-05 (0) | 3.928e-05 (0) | 3.351e-08 (0.59) |

So this is not an off-by-something error. Any closed ratio bound of this form is too loose when the roots are uninformed,
and even the tighter Z ≥ 1-m bound only gets to c≈1.6e-4.

## Suggested fixes (your call; both touch engine semantics)
- **(a) A stored compound queried as the goal returns its own stored count** when no other likelihood touches it. This matches
  the contract line "a source with no likelihoods remains a count". It is cheap and local, and gives the And query c=0.99,
  but the conjuncts stay at c≈0.
- **(b) Exact or numerical posterior moments for small nodes** (for example ≤4 atoms, polynomial in a few Betas: exact
  rational moments of N/Z by quadrature or series). This fixes the conjuncts (c≈0.54) but changes
  `docs/metta/event_node_contract.md:136`, which rules out quadrature and numerical refinement. It also costs time on
  larger nodes unless it is gated by size.
- (a) and (b) combine.

Downstream (wmtm/GoalChainer) records this as a known engine limit and carries no local patch.
